UNDERSTANDING MACROS

Macronutrients provide energy and support your body's functions.

PROTEIN

Protein helps build and repair muscles.

Foods: Chicken, eggs, fish, Greek yogurt, beans, and tofu.

CARBOHYDRATES

Carbohydrates provide energy for workouts.

Foods: Rice, oatmeal, potatoes, pasta, and fruit.

HEALTHY FATS

Fats support hormone production and overall health.

Foods: Avocados, nuts, olive oil, seeds, and salmon.

EATING FOR YOUR GOAL

BUILD MUSCLE

Eat enough calories and protein to support muscle growth and recovery.

GET LEANER

Focus on nutritious meals and regular exercise. A modest calorie deficit may support gradual fat loss.

MAINTAIN FITNESS

Eat balanced meals with protein, carbohydrates, healthy fats, fruits, and vegetables.

HEALTHY EATING TIPS

1. Drink water regularly throughout the day.

2. Include protein in your meals.

3. Eat fruits and vegetables daily.

4. Prepare meals ahead of time.

5. Choose affordable foods like oats, eggs, beans, and rice.

6. Eat enough to support your activity and recovery.
