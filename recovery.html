THE POWER OF SLEEP

Sleep supports muscle recovery, energy, and concentration.

MUSCLE RECOVERY

Your body repairs muscle tissue during rest. Getting enough sleep supports recovery.

MORE ENERGY

Consistent sleep helps you feel more energized during workouts and daily activities.

BETTER FOCUS

Sleep supports memory, concentration, and academic performance.

Most adults should aim for 7-9 hours of sleep each night.

WHY REST DAYS MATTER

MANAGE FATIGUE

Rest days help manage fatigue and reduce the risk of overuse injuries.

MUSCLE GROWTH

Muscles need time to recover between challenging training sessions.

ACTIVE RECOVERY

Light activities like walking, cycling, and stretching can help you stay active.

STRETCHING AND MOBILITY

BEFORE WORKOUTS

Try dynamic movements like arm circles, leg swings, and walking lunges.

AFTER WORKOUTS

Gentle static stretches can help maintain flexibility.

REST DAYS

Try light yoga or mobility exercises to maintain comfortable movement.

RECOVERY TIPS

1. Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep each night.

2. Drink water throughout the day.

3. Eat enough protein and balanced meals.

4. Include rest days in your routine.

5. Stretch regularly to improve flexibility.

6. Listen to your body and adjust when needed.

RECOVERY IS PROGRESS

Train hard, recover properly, and stay consistent. Your progress depends on both effort and rest.
